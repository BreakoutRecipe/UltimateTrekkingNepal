Trekking in Nepal

Himalayan Ultimate Trekking Nepal is one of the most reputed government-licensed trekking and touring agency in Nepal providing quality service to make each trek/tour a lifetime experience for all tourism holidays vacation trips into the Himalayas. Himalayan Ultimate Trekking Nepal offers a wide range of sight-seeing tours, trekking/expedition to bird watching and nature photography for the naturalist and outdoor enthusiast with a dedicated caring, reliable, friendly and experienced team. It holds a record of customer satisfaction in it’s over 12 years of experience in providing quality touring. Through its network of experienced field staffs- you will always be in good hands with Himalayan Ultimate Trekking Nepal. With long experience in organizing treks/tours for individuals and groups, Himalayan Ultimate Trekking Nepal can advise on the best treks/tours given the season, the region, your time and budget. You will find all trips at Himalayan Ultimate Trekking Nepal not only very competitive but also of the very highest standard. It’s ingredients are -Exceptional, hygienic and wholesome food on trek, caring and committed happy team, conscious and respect for the environment and culture.
