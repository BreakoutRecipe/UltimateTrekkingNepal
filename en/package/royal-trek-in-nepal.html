Royal Trek in Nepal

This is an easy and short trek that starts near Pokhara and offers beautiful views of the mountains. It gets its name because Prince Charles surrounded by 90 guests stayed here. This road is not very popular with trekkers. But it is a nice walk, not tiring with great insights into the Annapurna range. This six-day trek starts in Pokhara, the valley of lakes offering panoramic views of the Annapurna range but also on the Manaslu Himal. This is the best circuit for those who want to experience village life and enjoy the mountains in a relatively short time.
