Jomsom Muktinath Trek

This trip is a wonderful opportunity to witness the beauty of the Annapurna region with a shorter trek. The trek begins north of the city of Pokhara and winds through the Poon Hill area and then passes through the Dhaulagiri icefall giving a first view of the mountains. After reaching Jomsom Muktinath trek rises up to the Tibetan border, an important center of pilgrimage for Buddhists and Hindus.

This place gives an overview of the height and dry Tibetan Plateau. The trek ends with a flat path from Jomsom to Pokhara and Kathmandu. This is a trek that rises less and only 3800 m altitude. If you have limited time, you can simply take a flight to visit only Jomsom and Muktina.
